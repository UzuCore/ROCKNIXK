EXPERIMENTAL SOURCE CANDIDATE - NOT A COMPLETED OR HARDWARE-TESTED PORT

The u-boot recipe now packages Android-v4 boot, vendor_boot, dtbo and vbmeta
carriers. It is not a placeholder, and it does not build replacement SPL/U-Boot.
The built-in Linux initramfs is intended to load SYSTEM/STORAGE from microSD.
An independently installed compatible loader may use the extlinux payload too.

Installing Android-slot carriers would require an already unlocked bootloader,
verified device-specific backups and explicit authorization for inactive-slot
eMMC writes. No unlock, flash or eMMC write is performed by these preparation
tools. A stock PAC cannot replace the individual unit's NV/calibration backup.

Unchanged-stock SD-only entry and SD-removal rollback were an earlier proposed
policy, not the implemented entry mechanism. The preparation itself does not
write eMMC; a future inactive-slot installation requires separate authorization.
Do not repeatedly reboot an unconfirmed A/B slot.

Hardware nodes are enabled in this source candidate but have not been exercised.
Read SOURCE_GATES.md for implemented core fixes and validation boundaries.
Formal carrier verification does not prove actual bootloader acceptance,
graphics, audio, charging or suspend.
